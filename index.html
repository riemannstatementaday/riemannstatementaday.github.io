Riemann Statement a Day

Each day, one statement from the reading blueprint of Levent Alpöge's Lean proof of the Riemann Hypothesis. Read it against the Lean source, then send what you found: a check, a concern, or the place you got stuck. Every reading is logged here, misses included.

The statements, their labels and their links to Lean come from chrisflav/riemann-blueprint (read it at riemann.fuxi.merten.dev), with full credit. We aren't building a competing blueprint. The proof is Alpöge's. This page is run by volunteers and is not affiliated with him, Anthropic, or the Lean and Mathlib maintainers.

House rules

How a reading gets here

Send one from any statement, or from the Send a reading page; it becomes a public GitHub issue. We re-read it against the blueprint and the Lean source, reply there with our own verdict, and add it to the log. Readings that hit a wall are published too.

Say who did the work

Every reading records whether a person did it, a person with AI help, or an AI, and names the model. Readings done only by AI carry the "Unreviewed by humans" stamp until a person reads the same statement.

The paper

Checked readings and expositions are folded into one growing paper, in proof order. Only the maintainers write it, and every section names the statements it draws on.

What we don't do

  • Nothing here is a claim of priority or of a new result.
  • We never post to the blueprint, the Lean mirror or anyone's site on a reader's behalf. Issues belong in those projects' own trackers.
  • We link to the blueprint's text instead of copying it, and the Lean source carries no license, so we quote only declaration names.
